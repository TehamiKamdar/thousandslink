Faqs
Publisher Questions? Get the Answers Here
  • 01 Who can apply as a publisher?
    Website owners, bloggers and media publishers with original content and a genuine audience can apply. Every application is reviewed by our team before approval, so quality and relevance always come first.
  • 02 Do I have to accept every request?
    No. You choose which placements to accept. If a request does not fit your niche, your audience or your editorial standards, you can simply decline it. There is no pressure to publish anything you are not comfortable with.
  • 03 How and when do I get paid?
    Payment terms, schedules and payout methods are explained clearly during onboarding and before each placement, so you always know what to expect before you start.
  • 04 What kind of websites do you work with?
    We work with sites across many niches, as long as the content is original, the site is actively maintained and the audience is real. Contact us if you are unsure whether your niche fits.
  • 05 Will sponsored content hurt my website?
    It should not, when done right. We focus on relevant, well-written placements that fit your topic, and you review every request, so your readers keep getting content that matches your site.
  • 06 Can I choose which advertisers I work with?
    Yes. You see the details of each request before agreeing, and you can decline brands or topics that do not suit your website or your audience.
  • 07 Do I need technical skills to get started?
    No. If you can publish a post on your own website, you can work with us. Our team helps you through the process and answers your questions along the way.
  • 08 How do I track my placements?
    We keep you informed about the status of each placement, from request to publication to payout, so you always have a clear picture of your activity.

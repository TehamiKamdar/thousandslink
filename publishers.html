Faqs
Publisher Questions? Get the Answers Here
  • 01 Who can apply as a publisher?
    Website owners, bloggers and media publishers with original content and a genuine audience can apply. Every application is reviewed by our team before approval.
  • 02 Do I have to accept every request?
    No. You choose which placements to accept. If a request does not fit your niche or editorial standards, you can simply decline it.
  • 03 How and when do I get paid?
    Payment terms, schedules and available payout methods are shared clearly during onboarding, so you always know what to expect before you start.
  • 04 Does it cost anything to join?
    Applying and joining the network is free. Any commercial terms are explained upfront, before you accept a placement.
  • 05 What kind of websites do you work with?
    We work with sites across many niches, as long as the content is original, the site is actively maintained and the audience is real.

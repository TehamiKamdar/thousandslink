This policy describes what personal information ThousandsLink collects, why we collect it and the choices you have. We have written it in plain language, so you can easily understand how your information is used.

01.Who We Are

ThousandsLink is a platform that connects publishers and advertisers through relevant, quality-checked placements. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, apply as a publisher or work with us as an advertiser.

The data controller responsible for your personal information is [Legal Company Name], [Registered Address]. You can reach us at any time at support@thousandslink.com.

02.Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number and company name.
  • Publisher information: your website address, niche, audience and traffic details that you choose to share.
  • Advertiser information: your brand or agency details, campaign goals and budget range.
  • Messages: anything you write to us through our forms, email or phone.
  • Billing details: information needed to issue invoices and process payments or payouts.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited.
  • Cookie data: information collected through cookies and similar technologies (see Cookies below).
  • Preferences: your communication choices and newsletter subscription status.

03.How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for purposes that are connected to running and improving our services:

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the support you ask for.
  • To review publisher applications and assess whether a website fits our network.
  • To understand advertiser goals and recommend suitable publishers and placements.
  • To coordinate placements between publishers and advertisers.
  • To issue invoices, process payments and keep financial records.
  • To send service messages and, where permitted, newsletters or updates.
  • To keep our website secure, prevent fraud and fix technical problems.
  • To meet our legal obligations and protect our rights.

04.Legal Bases for Processing

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a similar region, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Contract: processing needed to provide the services you request or to take steps before entering into an agreement.
  • Consent: for example, when you subscribe to our newsletter or accept optional cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: running, securing and improving our platform, as long as your rights do not outweigh those interests.
  • Legal obligation: when we must keep records or respond to lawful requests.

05.How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We share it only in the following situations:

  • Between publishers and advertisers: only the details needed to arrange and deliver a placement.
  • Service providers: companies that help us with hosting, email, analytics, payments and customer support, under appropriate confidentiality terms.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when required by law, court order or to protect our rights, users or the public.
  • Business changes: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that process.
  • With your consent: in any other case where you have agreed to the sharing.

06.Cookies & Analytics

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We may use essential cookies to make the website work, and, where permitted, analytics or preference cookies to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the website work. Where the law requires consent for non-essential cookies, we will ask for it before using them.

07.Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting and reporting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Retention periods vary by type of data. For example, enquiry messages are kept for a limited time after the conversation ends, while invoices and payment records are kept for the period required by law.

08.Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. These include access controls, secure connections and careful selection of service providers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us right away.

09.International Transfers

Our team, partners and service providers may be located in different countries. This means your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect information that is transferred internationally.

10.Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to know what personal information we hold about you and to receive a copy.
  • The right to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • The right to ask us to delete your personal information.
  • The right to restrict how we use your information in certain cases.
  • The right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • The right to receive your information in a portable format.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@thousandslink.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

11.Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12.Third-Party Links

Our website and the content we help publish may contain links to other websites. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every site you visit.

13.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. When we make important changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, let you know in another way.

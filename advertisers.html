Faqs
Advertiser Questions? Get the Answers Here
  • 01 Who can advertise with ThousandsLink?
    Brands, agencies and marketers of all sizes can work with us, as long as the product or service is legitimate and fits the standards of our publishers.
  • 02 How do you choose publishers for my campaign?
    We match your campaign with vetted publishers based on niche, topic, audience and content quality, then share the proposed placements for your approval before anything goes live.
  • 03 How is pricing decided?
    Pricing depends on the placement type, the publisher and the scope of your campaign. We share a clear quote before any work begins, so there are no surprises later.
  • 04 Can I see the results of my campaign?
    Yes. We provide clear reporting on your placements so you can review progress, share it with your team or clients and decide how to optimize.
  • 05 Do I need a large budget to start?
    No. You can start with a focused campaign and scale up as you see results. Contact us and we will suggest a plan that fits your goals and budget.
  • 06 Who writes the content for my placements?
    This depends on the campaign. We can work with content you provide or help arrange content that fits the publisher’s style. You review it before publication either way.
  • 07 Can agencies manage several clients with you?
    Yes. Agencies are welcome. We can plan separate campaigns for each client and provide reporting that is easy to share.
  • 08 How long does it take to launch a campaign?
    Timelines depend on the scope of the campaign and publisher availability. We share a realistic timeline with your plan, so you know what to expect.

Faqs
Have Questions in Your Mind? Get the Answers Now
  • 01 What is ThousandsLink?
    ThousandsLink is a platform that connects publishers and advertisers. Publishers earn from their websites, and advertisers reach the right audience through relevant, quality-checked placements.
  • 02 Who can join as a publisher?
    Website owners, bloggers and media publishers with original content and a genuine audience can apply. Every application is reviewed by our team before approval.
  • 03 Who can advertise with ThousandsLink?
    Brands, agencies and marketers of all sizes can work with us, as long as the product or service is legitimate and fits the standards of our publishers.
  • 04 How does matching work?
    We match requests by niche, topic, audience and content quality, then share the details with both sides so each placement is approved before anything goes live.
  • 05 Do publishers have to accept every request?
    No. Publishers choose which placements to accept and can decline anything that does not suit their website, audience or editorial standards.
  • 06 How is pricing decided?
    Pricing depends on the placement type, the publisher and the scope of the campaign. A clear quote is shared before any work begins, so there are no surprises later.
  • 07 Can I see the results of my campaign?
    Yes. We provide clear updates and reporting on placements, so advertisers can review progress and publishers can follow the status of every request.
  • 08 How do I get started?
    Visit the Publishers or Advertisers page, or send us a message through the contact form. Our team will guide you through the next steps.

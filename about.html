About us
We Help Publishers and Advertisers Grow Together
Thousandslink was built around one simple idea: publishers and advertisers do better when they can find each other easily and work on fair, transparent terms. Many website owners struggle to earn from their traffic without hurting reader trust, while many brands struggle to find quality websites for their message. We close that gap. Our team reviews websites and campaigns, matches them by niche and audience, and coordinates every placement, so both sides know what to expect from the first request to the final report.

Faqs
Curious About Us? Find the Answers Here
  • 01 What does Thousandslink do?
    We connect publishers and advertisers. Publishers earn from their websites, and advertisers reach the right audience through relevant, quality-checked placements that both sides approve.
  • 02 How do you keep quality high?
    Our team reviews every publisher application and every advertiser request. We look at content quality, relevance and fit before suggesting a placement, and both sides see the details before anything goes live.
  • 03 Do you work with small businesses and small websites?
    Yes. Size matters less than relevance, quality and honesty. If your website or brand is a good fit for what we do, we are happy to talk.
  • 04 Can I talk to someone before I decide?
    Of course. Send us a message through the contact form, email us or call +92 3800 8060, and a member of our team will get back to you and answer your questions.
  • 05 How is my information handled?
    We collect only what we need to respond to you and to coordinate placements, and we do not sell personal information.
  • 06 How can I become a partner?
    If you own a website, apply on the Publishers page. If you want to promote a brand, start on the Advertisers page. You can also contact us directly.
